Profiles list sections in a **preferred** order, such as Introduction → Methods → Results → Discussion. Many papers deviate for good reasons: a theory paper may state its main result first and prove it later, and a review is organised by topic.

In Template mode, Lemma mentions when your order differs from the profile, at **info** level only. Never reorder just to satisfy a template; reorder if it makes the argument easier to follow.
