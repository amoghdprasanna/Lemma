A common shape for an introduction is **context → gap → contribution → roadmap**:

1. **Context.** What is the field and why does it matter? Cite the key work.
2. **Gap.** What is missing, unsolved or unclear?
3. **Contribution.** What exactly does this paper add? Be concrete.
4. **Roadmap.** One short paragraph: "Section II introduces…, Section III presents…, and Section IV discusses…".

The roadmap helps readers of longer papers jump to what they need. Short Letters often skip it.

**Style choice.** This is a widely used pattern, not a requirement. Lemma only mentions a missing roadmap as a learning note.
