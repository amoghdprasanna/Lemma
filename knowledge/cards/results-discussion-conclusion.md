These three are easy to blur together. A useful split:

- **Results** says _what you found_, as plainly as possible: the theorem, the measured threshold, the figure that shows the scaling.
- **Discussion** says _what it means_: interpretation, limitations, assumptions that might fail, and how the result compares with prior work.
- **Conclusion / Outlook** says _the take-away and what comes next_, in a few sentences. Do not just repeat the abstract.

Some papers merge Results and Discussion, or Discussion and Outlook. That is fine when the paper is short.

**Style choice.** Lemma reasons about these roles, not the headings, and only raises a warning in Template mode when your chosen profile marks a role as required.
