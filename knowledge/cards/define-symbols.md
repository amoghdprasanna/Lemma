Every symbol in a displayed equation should be introduced in the text, before the equation or right after it:

> The Hamiltonian is $H = -\sum_v A_v - \sum_p B_p$, where $A_v$ and $B_p$ are the vertex and plaquette operators.

Readers should never have to guess what a symbol means, or hunt for its definition.

Lemma's check is a heuristic: it looks for symbols that appear in displayed equations but never in the running text, and reports them as learning notes. Standard constants such as $\pi$, $e$ and $i$ are exempt. Your notation sheet shows what your paper does introduce.
