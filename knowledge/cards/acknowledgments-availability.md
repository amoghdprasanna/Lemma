**Acknowledgments** credit funding (with grant numbers), collaborators who are not authors, and computing resources. REVTeX provides an `acknowledgments` environment for this.

**Data and code availability** statements tell readers where they can find the data and code behind your results, for example a repository with a persistent identifier. Many publishers now ask for one; requirements vary by journal.

**Style choice.** Whether these are required depends on your journal and funder. Lemma marks them optional in its built-in profiles.
